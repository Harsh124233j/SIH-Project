Best Destinations to Visit in India

Goa

The unofficial party place of India, Goa is more than that. It has a great legacy, history and culture that are yet to be explored. But if beaches are what you are looking for, then the state has that too. And that's why, India tours to Goa are a great option to explore the place, according to your interests. Goa holiday packages are also popular because Goa is one of the top places in the world when it comes to nightlife. Find several exciting tour packages and other India holiday packages that let you try Goa's melange of watersports and other fun activities.


Best time to visit: November to February


Best places to visit:

  • Calangute
  • Baga
  • Anjuna
  • Miramar
  • Palolem
  • Panjim
You can also include the offbeat Patnem Beach in your holiday tour packages, since it was listed amongst one of Asia's top 20 beaches.

Kerala

God's own country, Kerala has been a popular tourist destination in India. You can explore it all with India tours to this marvellous destination. With suitable tour packages, you can discover scenic seaside destinations and hill stations in Kerala. Whether you want to cruise on a traditional houseboat in Alleppey, witness vibrant Kathakali dance performances, or rejuvenate with authentic Ayurvedic wellness treatments, Kerala holiday packages cater to all kinds of travelers. Find several exciting tour packages and other India tour packages that let you experience Kerala's rich heritage, spice plantations, and mouth-watering coastal gastronomy.


Best time to visit: September to March


Best places to visit:

  • Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple
  • Francis CSI Church
  • Paradesi Synagogue
  • Eravikulam National Park
  • Varkala Beach
  • Athirappilly Waterfalls
If you are planning India holiday packages in the state, then you must not miss out on its lip-smacking cuisine as well. Check your holiday tour packages and book accordingly.

Kashmir

For decades, the Kashmir valley has attracted tourists from all over the world. If you can't plan a vacation on your own, you should check out India tours for Kashmir to help you with an unforgettable holiday. With the right all India tour packages, you can discover lush green valleys, snow-covered peaks and gorgeous wildlife. Your trip packages must include the Chenab, Sindh and Lidder valleys. Don't forget to buy a world-famous Kashmiri shawl and one-of-a-kind handicrafts for your loved ones.


Best Time to Visit: March to August


Best Places to Visit:

  • Gulmarg
  • Srinagar
  • Dal Lake

You can also include the offbeat Gurez Valley, Doodhpathri, Bangus Valley, and Sinthan Top in your holiday tour packages, since they were listed amongst some of the most serene and untouched high-altitude hidden gems in Kashmir.

Rajasthan

If you are looking for mesmerising India tours, then Rajasthan is undoubtedly your destination of choice. From its sprawling forts and historical landmarks to its signature architecture, beautiful lakes and rich culinary traditions, the state is a must-try for the best tour packages in the country. Your trip packages should include visits to several historical monuments.


Best Time to Visit: November to February


Best Places to Visit:

  • Jaipur
  • Udaipur
  • Chittor

You can also include the offbeat Abhaneri Stepwell, Bhangarh Fort, Khuri Village, and Bundi in your holiday tour packages, since they were listed amongst some of the most extraordinary historical and cultural hidden gems in Rajasthan.

Agra

Home to the world-famous Taj Mahal, Agra offers an unforgettable glimpse into rich Mughal history, architecture, and culture. Visitors can explore majestic forts, grand tombs, and bustling heritage bazaars through tailored travel experiences.


Best time to visit: October to March


Best places to visit:

  • Taj Mahal
  • Agra Fort
  • Fatehpur Sikri
  • Itmad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)
  • Mehtab Bagh
  • Akbar's Tomb
You can also include the offbeat Mehtab Bagh sunset viewpoint in your holiday tour packages, since it was listed amongst one of the best locations for panoramic views of the Taj Mahal.

Mumbai

The bustling City of Dreams, Mumbai is a grand fusion of colonial architecture, vibrant street life, iconic coastal drives, and rich Bollywood culture. From historic landmarks overlooking the Arabian Sea to bustling local markets, the city offers dynamic travel options for every explorer.


Best time to visit: October to February


Best places to visit:

  • Gateway of India
  • Marine Drive
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus
  • Elephanta Caves
  • Colaba Causeway
  • Juhu Beach
You can also include the offbeat Banganga Tank in your holiday tour packages, since it was listed amongst one of the most serene historical hidden gems in South Mumbai.
