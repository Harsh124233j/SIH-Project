Kerala

Kerala is a coastal state located on the tropical Malabar Coast of southwestern India, along the Arabian Sea. It is known for its serene palm-fringed backwaters, rich cultural traditions, and diverse natural landscapes, including lush tea plantations, mist-covered Western Ghats mountains, and rich wildlife sanctuaries

Varanasi

The Varanasi Ghats are a series of historic stone riverfront steps lining the sacred Ganges River in Uttar Pradesh, northern India. They are known for their profound spiritual significance, ancient temples and palaces, and vibrant daily rituals, including early-morning holy dips, centuries-old cremation rites, and the evening Ganga Aarti ceremonies..

Kashmir

Kashmir is a picturesque valley region located in the northernmost part of the Indian subcontinent, cradled between the Great Himalayas and the Pir Panjal mountain range. It is known for its iconic wooden shikaras drifting on Dal Lake, Mughal-era terraced gardens, and breathtaking natural landscape and forests.

Goa

Goa is a coastal state located in southwestern India along the Konkan region, bordering the Arabian Sea. It is known for its sun-drenched golden beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese colonial-era architecture and historic churches, and lush tropical landscapes featuring spice plantations and scenic waterfalls.
